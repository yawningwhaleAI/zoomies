Why this problem

Pet food shouldn't need a lab report to be trusted. You add one anyway.

Most pet food sells comfort: shiny packaging over by-products, fillers and fuzzy labels. Zoomies is betting that clean, meat-first food can be shelf-stable, affordable and scalable at the same time. That's not a branding problem. It's an operating and unit-economics problem, and that's the part I want to own.

I resonate with it for three reasons. I'm a pet lover, so what goes into the bowl matters to me. I've run a D2C dropshipping store myself, so I know how CAC and ad creative feel from the inside. And I spent three years where every claim had to survive a backtest before real money went in. A lab-report QR on every pack is the same instinct.

A true story

Meet Gabbar.

Before I ever read a balance sheet, a tiny abandoned puppy taught me that what goes into the bowl changes everything.

  1. 01 · Found

    Abandoned and alone

    In college, some of my hostel friends and I found a puppy who had been abandoned and left on his own. He was tiny, weak and had nobody coming back for him. So we decided he had us.

  2. 02 · Named

    A big name for a small dog

    We called him Gabbar. Any puppy strong enough to survive on his own, with no family and no food, had earned the toughest name we knew.

  3. 03 · The vet

    Low on vitamins, high on spirit

    We took him to a vet. He was low on vitamins, so we got him vaccinated and started feeding him properly, with real food.

  4. 04 · N Block

    A whole hostel block of parents

    Gabbar became the hostel's puppy. We split the feeding and the check-ins, and I posted a request for the whole of N Block to look after him.

    "He is low on vitamins right now, got him vaccinated, all the N block people please look after him." My Instagram story, back then
  5. 05 · Grew

    From the box to the bowl

    Then we got to watch him grow: from a sleepy bundle in a box into a puppy who attacked his food bowl every single time.

What Gabbar taught me

The fastest way to see a pet get better is to fix what's in the bowl.

We had no money and no experience, just a vet's advice and better food. It worked. That's why "real food, clear labels" isn't just a tagline to me, and why Zoomies' mission is one I'd genuinely want to work for.
